Features

Everything happens in three taps

No typing kanji on your phone. No copy-pasting between apps. No desktop required.

Photo Capture

Point your camera at any textbook page, vocab list, or handwritten notes. MyStudyDeck captures the image and runs OCR instantly — works offline, no network needed.

Smart OCR

VisionKit extracts Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Arabic, and more. Individual terms are tokenized into selectable chips.

Auto-Translation

Apple's Translation framework translates selected terms into your target language automatically. 10 source languages, 13 targets.

Card Builder

Four card types — Plain, Image, Cloze, and Multi-field. Batch mode lets you build multiple cards from one photo.

.apkg Export

Generates standard Anki-compatible .apkg files with embedded images. Export via Share Sheet to AnkiMobile, Files, AirDrop, iCloud Drive, or email. No desktop required.

Privacy

Privacy is not a feature.
It's the architecture.

On-device OCR

Photos are processed by Apple's VisionKit on your phone. They never touch a server.

On-device translation

Apple's Translation framework runs locally. Your text is never sent to external translation APIs.

No accounts, no cloud

All data lives in a local SQLite database. No sign-up. No syncing. Delete the app and it's gone.

No ad tracking

Zero ads, zero ad-tracking SDKs. Anonymous analytics only (TelemetryDeck). We never see what you study.

How It Works
