1. What MyStudyDeck does

MyStudyDeck is an iOS app that turns photos of study material into Anki-compatible flashcard decks (.apkg files). It runs entirely on your device — no accounts, no cloud storage, no server.

2. Free tier and Pro purchase

Free tier: You can create up to 3 flashcards at no cost. Export is not available on the free tier.

Pro (one-time purchase, $14.99 USD): Unlocks unlimited card creation and .apkg export. This is a one-time purchase — no subscription, no recurring charges. Once purchased, Pro access is yours permanently on the Apple ID used for purchase.

Pricing may vary by region based on Apple's App Store pricing. All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not handle payment information directly.

3. Restoring purchases

If you reinstall the app or switch devices, you can restore your Pro purchase using the "Restore Purchase" option in the app. This uses Apple's StoreKit to verify your prior purchase. We do not manage purchase records — Apple does.
